🌳 The Core Mission

Protect the Trees of Life. Enemies come out of the Portals and march along the path toward them.

  1. 1Pick a tower - click a tower card at the bottom of the screen (or press 1 - 9).
  2. 2Build it - click free ground next to the path. The tower shoots every enemy in its range.
  3. 3Grow - every kill gives you Gold for more towers. When it gets tough, use an Ability.
  • LivesEvery enemy that reaches a Tree takes your Lives - 1, or the number on its damage indicator (⚔️). At 0 Lives the game is over.
  • ❤️HealersEnemies with a heal indicator (❤️) give you Lives when they reach a Tree instead of taking them.
  • 🪙GoldEarned by destroying enemies, spent on towers.

Game Flow

How a map is played from the first enemy to the last:

  • WavesEvery map is a series of waves. The next wave starts on its own as soon as every enemy of the current one is gone - the progress bar at the top shows how far the wave is.
  • VictorySurvive every wave with at least one ❤️ Life left. If your Lives drop to 0, it's a Defeat.
  • Extra LifeThe +1 Life button next to Towers / Abilities (or the E key) buys a life for gold. Every purchase costs more - and some maps don't offer it at all.
  • Game SpeedThe Speed buttons in the top bar run the game from 1x up to 10x.
  • LogThe Log button in the top bar lists everything that happened - every wave, towers built and sold, abilities used, Lives bought and the real damage / heal of enemies with an indicator.
  • Tower CardsEvery card shows its Price, Sell value and DPS - hover it to see all six stats. The number in its corner is its 1 - 9 hotkey.
💡
Tip: Hold SHIFT while placing a tower to see its range before you build it.
💡
Tip: An indicator showing "+99" is capped - the real value (it can be 1,000 or more) is written to the Logs as soon as such an enemy appears, and the Log button shakes a little.

Tower Mechanics

Understanding your defenses is key to survival:

  • 🪙PriceThe cost to build the tower.
  • 💥DPSDamage Per Second. This represents the average total damage dealt every second.
  • ⚔️DamageThe amount of health removed from an enemy with a single hit.
  • 🎯RangeThe circle around the tower in which it shoots at enemies. An arrow already fired keeps chasing its enemy even out of range until it hits - only a mountain, tree or rock in its way can stop it.
  • ⏱️Fire RateThe delay between shots, measured in milliseconds (ms).
    (Note: 400ms is faster than 800ms because the tower waits less time between attacks!)
  • 🗲SpeedThe projectile speed (how fast the arrows or bullets travel to reach the target).
  • 💰Sell PriceThe amount of gold you get back for removing the tower.
Left-click to Build the selected tower on free ground - grass, snow, sand or dirt (not on the path, water, mountains, lava or objects). Click the selected card again (or press its number) to cancel the selection.
Right-click a tower to Dismantle it and recover some gold.

Enemies

Every map sends its own mix of enemies - they differ in health, speed and the gold they drop.

  • 🩸HealthThe bar above every enemy shows how much health it has left - yellow above half, red below.
  • 🗲SpeedFast enemies spend less time in your towers' range - they need more towers or a faster Fire Rate.
  • 🪙RewardEvery enemy type drops its own amount of Gold when destroyed.
  • ⚔️DamageAn enemy with a damage indicator takes that many Lives if it reaches a Tree.
  • ❤️HealersAn enemy with a heal indicator gives you that many Lives when it reaches a Tree.
  • BossesSome maps have bosses - tough enemies with lots of health. On some maps the screen shakes when a boss enters. Get your strongest towers and abilities ready!
💡
Tip: Mix your towers - hard single hits for enemies with lots of health, a fast Fire Rate for quick ones.

Abilities

Special powers granted by the Elder Mages - every map gives you its own set.

  • 🎯TargetedSelect the card, then click a path tile - the effect covers a stretch of path around it (the card shows how many tiles). Click the card again to cancel.
  • 🗺️GlobalStarts the moment you click the card and affects the whole map.
  • 🕒DurationHow long the effect lasts. While it runs, the card shows Active with a shrinking bar.
  • ⏳CooldownHow long until you can use it again. It starts the moment you use the ability - the duration counts into it - and the dimmed card counts down.

(Note: Abilities may carry a different name on each map - the effect stays the same as described on the next pages.)

💡
Tip: Both times are on every ability card (🕒 duration, ⏳ cooldown). Every ability page below shows whether it is Targeted or Global.

🌋 Ability: Lava Floor Targeted

A powerful area-of-effect (AoE) attack that scorches the earth.

  • UsageSelect the ability and click a path tile.
  • EffectSets a stretch of path around the chosen tile on fire and deals rapid damage to every enemy standing on the burning tiles.
  • TimingFire it when a big group of enemies is about to walk over the chosen tiles.
⚠️
Important: Overlapping Lava Floors add up - every one of them deals its own damage.
💡
Hint: Best used on "choke points" where enemies group together!

🏹 Ability: Tower's Fury Global

A global buff that pushes your towers beyond their limits.

  • UsageSelect the ability - it activates instantly, no tile needs to be chosen.
  • EffectTemporarily increases the Damage and Speed of ALL towers on the map and shortens the delay between their shots (Fire Rate) - they fire faster and hit significantly harder while it lasts.
  • TimingSave this for massive waves or Boss enemies!
⚠️
Important: Two different Tower's Fury abilities active at once multiply (+50% and +50% Damage = +125%, not +100%).

⛏️ Ability: Gold Rush Global

A global economy boost that makes every kill worth more.

  • UsageSelect the ability - it activates instantly, no tile needs to be chosen.
  • EffectWhile active, gold earned from killing enemies is increased by a percentage bonus (e.g. +100% = double gold).
  • LimitsThe bonus does not apply to gold from selling towers.
  • TimingActivate it right before a big wave to fund new towers!
⚠️
Important: Two different Gold Rush abilities active at once add up (+100% and +100% = +200%).

❄️ Ability: Ice Storm Targeted

A freezing storm that turns a stretch of the path into ice.

  • UsageSelect the ability and click a path tile.
  • EffectFreezes a stretch of path around the chosen tile - enemies on the ice move much slower (e.g. -80% speed) and take extra damage from all sources - towers and Lava Floor alike (e.g. +50%: a 50 damage hit deals 75).
  • LimitsThe effect lasts only while an enemy stands on the ice. Frozen enemies glow blue.
  • TimingFreeze the path inside the range of your strongest towers.
⚠️
Important: Overlapping Ice Storms don't add up - the strongest slow and the strongest vulnerability apply.
💡
Hint: Combine it with Lava Floor - enemies stay in the fire longer and burn harder!
