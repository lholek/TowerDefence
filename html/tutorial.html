🛡️ The Core Mission

Protect the Tree of Life. Enemies emerge from Portals (S) and march toward the Exit (E).

💡 Tip: Every enemy that escapes reduces your ❤️ 10 Lives. If you reach 0, the game is over.
  • 🪙 Gold: Earned by destroying enemies.
  • 🏰 Strategy: Place towers on green tiles to maximize their firing time.

🏹 Tower Mechanics

Understanding your defenses is key to survival:

  • Range: The circular area where the tower can detect enemies.
  • Fire Rate: Shown in milliseconds (ms).
    (Note: 400ms is faster than 800ms because the delay between shots is shorter!)
  • Damage: The health removed from an enemy per hit.
Right-click a tower to Dismantle it and recover some gold.

🌋 Ability: Lava Floor

A powerful area-of-effect (AoE) attack that scorches the earth.

  • Usage: Select the ability and click a path tile.
  • Effect: Sets the selected tile (and nearby tiles) on fire.
  • Damage: Deals rapid damage to every enemy standing on the burning tiles.

Best used on "choke points" where enemies group together!

🏹 Ability: Tower's Fury

A global buff that pushes your towers beyond their limits.

  • Effect: Temporarily reduces the Fire Rate of ALL towers on the map.
  • Power: Your towers will shoot significantly faster for the duration of the spell.
  • Timing: Save this for massive waves or Boss enemies!
