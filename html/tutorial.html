🌳 The Core Mission

Protect the Tree of Life. Enemies emerge from Portals and march toward the Tress.

💡 Tip: Every enemy that escapes reduces your ❤️ Lives. If it deals more that 1 damage its identified by damage indicator (⚔️). If you reach 0, the game is over.
💡 Tip: If the damage indicator (⚔️) displays "+99", it doesn't show the real value. The actual damage could be 1,000 or more, as the indicator caps at 100.
  • 🪙 Gold: Earned by destroying enemies.
  • 🏰 Strategy: Place towers to defend the trees.
  • 🌋 Strategy: Use abilites to defend the trees.

🏹 Tower Mechanics

Understanding your defenses is key to survival:

  • Price: The cost to build the tower.
  • DPS: Damage Per Second. This represents the average total damage dealt every second.
  • Damage: The amount of health removed from an enemy with a single hit.
  • Range: The circular area within which the tower can detect and attack enemies.
  • Fire Rate: The delay between shots, measured in milliseconds (ms).
    (Note: 400ms is faster than 800ms because the tower waits less time between attacks!)
  • Speed: The projectile speed (how fast the arrows or bullets travel to reach the target).
  • Sell Price: The amount of gold you get back for removing the tower.
Left-click to Build selected a tower on grass.
Right-click a tower to Dismantle it and recover some gold.

🌋 Ability: Lava Floor

A powerful area-of-effect (AoE) attack that scorches the earth.

  • Usage: Select the ability and click a path tile.
  • Effect: Sets the selected tile (and nearby tiles) on fire.
  • Damage: Deals rapid damage to every enemy standing on the burning tiles.

Best used on "choke points" where enemies group together!

🏹 Ability: Tower's Fury

A global buff that pushes your towers beyond their limits.

  • Effect: Temporarily increses the Demage and Speed and reduces the Fire Rate of ALL towers on the map.
  • Power: Your towers will shoot significantly faster and stronger for the duration of the ability.
  • Timing: Save this for massive waves or Boss enemies!
